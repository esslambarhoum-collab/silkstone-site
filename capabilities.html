Capabilities

Fabric in.
Garments out.

Seven stages, one building, our own supervision throughout. Nothing is subcontracted, so nothing is untracked.

01

Fabric sourcing

Yarn and knitted fabric from Egyptian mills and partner mills abroad. Country of origin declared for all cotton.

02

Fabric inspection

100% of incoming rolls through the inspection machine for defects, shade and weight before release.

03

Cutting

Computerised auto-cutting. Marker, plies and consumption logged against the order.

04

Sewing

Sixteen lines running, thirty-three at full capacity. Hourly output by operation.

05

Trims

Labels, drawcords, eyelets, snaps and buttons. Pull tests on every accessory batch.

06

Print and embroidery

Rotary and continuous printing and an embroidery department, in house, alongside the sewing floor.

07

Packing and dispatch

Final random inspection, metal detection, export-ready cartons.

Materials

Cotton at the source.

Egypt spins and knits within reach of the factory. We source jersey, fleece and loopback from Egyptian mills and from partner mills through Aletex, and we can supply Better Cotton, organic and recycled fibre with transaction certificates.

  • Cotton jersey single jersey, interlock, rib, in weights to your tech pack
  • Fleece and loopback brushed and unbrushed, cotton and cotton blends
  • Certified fibre GOTS organic, OCS, RCS and GRS recycled, with scope and transaction certificates
  • Origin declared for all raw cotton, no restricted origins
Equipment and departments

What is on the floor.

Cutting
Computerised auto-cutting line. Digital markers, logged consumption per lay.
Sewing
16 active lines, 33 at full capacity. Output recorded hourly by operation, visible in the ERP.
Printing
In-house automatic printing department, rotary and continuous. Screen and transfer work handled alongside.
Embroidery
Embroidery department in house, so branded basics do not leave the building.
Fabric inspection
Fabric inspection machine, 100% of incoming rolls tested for defects and colour consistency.
Product safety
Calibrated metal detector and a secured inspection zone before final packing. Broken needle tracking and replacement policy. Button and snap pull tester.
Sampling
Dedicated sample development department for prototypes and fast approvals.
Systems
Integrated ERP across every stage. Order-based tracking, inline and final QC recorded digitally, client access to production status and quality reports.
Orders

How we take work.

Minimum order 20,000 pieces per style. A first trial order can run at 10,000. Lead times are quoted per order once fabric is confirmed, and a full production plan is shared before the first cut.

CM
Cut and make. You supply fabric and trims, we cut, sew and finish.
CMT
Cut, make and trim. You supply fabric, we source trims.
FOB
Full package. We source fabric and trims to your specification and deliver to port.
Sampling
Proto, fit, pre-production and shipment samples, sent for approval at each stage.
