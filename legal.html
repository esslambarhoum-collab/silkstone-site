Privacy policy

Who we are

This website is operated by Silkstone Fashion, Plot No. 236, 6 Million Area, Southern Industrial Zone, 10th of Ramadan City, Egypt. Commercial register number and tax registration number to be added. For anything about your data, write to info@silkstone-textile.com.

What we collect

  • What you send us through the contact form or the tech pack generator: your name, company, email address, phone number if you give it, and the details of your enquiry. Both tools open your own email application, so the message is sent by you, from your account, and we receive it as an ordinary email.
  • Drafts you save in the tech pack generator are stored in your browser only, on your device. They are not sent to us until you choose to send them.
  • Standard server logs: the pages requested, the time, and the IP address of the request, kept for security and error diagnosis.

Why we use it

To answer your enquiry, prepare a quotation or sample, arrange a visit, and keep a record of our correspondence with you as a prospective or actual customer. We do not sell or rent your information, and we do not use it for advertising.

Who else sees it

Fonts on this site are loaded from Google Fonts, and the 3D viewer on the products page is loaded from the jsDelivr content delivery network. Your browser requests those files directly, which shares your IP address with those services under their own privacy policies. No analytics or advertising scripts run on this site. Update if analytics or a form service is added.

How long we keep it

Enquiry correspondence is kept for as long as we are in contact with you and for a reasonable period afterwards for business records. Server logs are kept for no more than ninety days.

Your rights

You can ask us what we hold about you, ask for it to be corrected or deleted, and object to how we use it, by writing to the address above. If you are in the European Union or the United Kingdom, you also have the right to complain to your local data protection authority.
