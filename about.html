About

Two partners.
One factory.

Silkstone Fashion is a knitwear manufacturer in 10th of Ramadan City, Egypt, owned equally by Boldbridge Capital and Aletex Group. European buying discipline and Egyptian production depth, under one roof.

The company

Built to be audited.

Silkstone runs sixteen sewing lines today with room for thirty-three, across a floor that holds fabric inspection, computerised cutting, sewing, rotary and continuous printing, embroidery, metal detection and packing. Nothing in the chain leaves the building.

The company is registered as Silkstone Fashion and trades as Silkstone Textile. Its clients include H&M, DeFacto and LC Waikiki, and retailers in the United States. It holds WRAP, Sedex, ISO 9001, ISO 14001, ISO 45001, GOTS, OCS, RCS and GRS.

The partners

Who stands behind the floor.

Boldbridge Capital

A UK-headquartered group founded in 2005, operating in Egypt, Saudi Arabia and the United Arab Emirates. Its businesses span manufacturing of fibre optic cables and precast concrete, infrastructure and engineering, smart city development and strategic investment. The group is led by Hany Naeem, a member of the Forbes Business Council with more than twenty-five years building infrastructure across the Middle East and Africa.

boldbridge.co.uk

Aletex Group

A circular knitwear group based in Canet de Mar, Barcelona, founded in 2008. Aletex designs and produces knitted collections for global fashion brands, from trend research and concept development to the finished product, and works with Zara, Bershka, Pull&Bear, Massimo Dutti, H&M, Urban Outfitters and Anthropologie among others. Its own factories and strategic partners give Silkstone its European buying discipline and its route into Spanish retail.
