Two partners. One factory.

Silkstone Fashion is a knitwear manufacturer in Egypt, owned equally by Boldbridge Capital and Aletex Group. Discipline from Barcelona, depth from Egypt, under one roof.

Photograph to follow
Factory exterior with signage

Made to be checked.

Sixteen sewing lines today with room for thirty-three, on a floor that holds fabric inspection, computerised cutting, sewing, rotary and continuous printing, embroidery, metal detection and packing. Nothing in the chain leaves the building. Clients include H&M, DeFacto and LC Waikiki, and retailers in the United States, and the factory holds WRAP, Sedex, three ISO standards and four fibre certifications.

Who stands behind the floor.

Boldbridge Capital

A UK-headquartered group founded in 2005, operating in Egypt, Saudi Arabia and the United Arab Emirates. Its businesses span fibre optic cable and precast concrete manufacturing, infrastructure and engineering, smart city development and strategic investment. The group is led by Hany Naeem, a member of the Forbes Business Council with more than twenty-five years building infrastructure across the Middle East and Africa.

boldbridge.co.uk

Aletex Group

A circular knitwear group based in Canet de Mar, Barcelona, founded in 2008. Aletex designs and produces knitted collections for global fashion brands, from trend research and concept development to the finished product, working with Zara, Bershka, Pull&Bear, Massimo Dutti, H&M, Urban Outfitters and Anthropologie among others. It gives Silkstone its European buying discipline and its route into Spanish retail.

aletex.net
